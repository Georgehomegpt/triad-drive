Privacy Policy — TRIAD Drive

TRIAD Drive is a personal tool used by a single account owner to synchronize files between a private Raspberry Pi 5 and that owner's own Google Drive.

The application accesses only the Google Drive of the account owner who authorizes it. It reads and writes files solely within that Drive.

No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. No data is stored outside the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own device.

The application has no users other than its owner. Access can be revoked at any time in the owner's Google Account settings.
